Welcome to the Constant

Don't Starve Together is the standalone multiplayer expansion of the uncompromising wilderness survival game, Don't Starve. Enter a strange and unexplored world full of odd creatures, dangers, and surprises. Gather resources to craft items and structures that match your survival style. Play your way as you unravel the mysteries of this strange land.

Cooperate with your friends in a private game, or take your chances with strangers online. Work with other players to survive the harsh environment, or strike out on your own. Do whatever it takes, but most importantly: Don't Starve!

Essential Survival Goals:

  • Day 1-5: Gather flint and twigs to make your first pickaxe and axe.
  • Day 6-10: Find a good spot for a base near Beefalo or Pig Village.
  • Day 11-20: Prepare for Winter by crafting warm clothing and storing food.

Game Statistics Definition:

Health
Represents your physical condition. If it hits zero, you die.
Hunger
Drains constantly. You must eat regularly to stay alive.
Sanity
Represents your mental state. Low sanity brings shadow monsters to life.
