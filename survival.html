Beginner's Survival Guide

The key is to manage your time wisely between gathering resources and preparing for the night. Follow these tips to ensure your campfire stays lit and your stomach stays full.

Your First Priorities:

  1. Collect 40 Grass and 40 Twigs.
  2. Find Flint for tools.
  3. Locate food:
    1. Berries.
    2. Carrots.
    3. Rabbits.

Nighttime Essentials:

  • Always have a Torch.
  • Build a Campfire.
  • Stay close to the light.

Core Mechanics:

The Darkness
Complete darkness will kill you instantly.
Seasons
Prepare for Winter and Summer.
