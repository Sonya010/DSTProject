How to Fight Hostile Mobs

The world of the Constant is filled with dangerous creatures that will attack you on sight. To survive, you must learn the "kiting" technique: hitting an enemy and moving away before they strike back. Below is a summary of the most common threats and epic bosses you will encounter.

Common Hostile Mobs
Spider Hound Treeguard
Spider Wiki Hound Wiki Treeguard Wiki
Found in Spider Dens. Attack during the day or when the nest is hit. Use traps to catch them easily. Attack in waves every few days. Run to Beefalo or Pigs for help if you are overwhelmed. Spawn when you chop too many trees. Can be pacified by planting pinecones near them.


Epic Seasonal Bosses
Deerclops Bearger Dragonfly

Winter Boss. Destroys structures and has a freezing aura. Kite him away from your main base. Autumn Giant. Consumes food and uproots trees. Can be used to "farm" wood before fighting him. Year-round Boss found in the desert. Very high health. Requires a dedicated arena and armor.
