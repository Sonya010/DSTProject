Beginner's Survival Guide

Main | Characters | Monsters | How to survive | Official Site

Surviving the first few days in Don't Starve Together can be challenging for new players. The key is to manage your time wisely between gathering resources and preparing for the night. Follow these tips to ensure your campfire stays lit and your stomach stays full.

Your First Priorities:

  1. Collect at least 40 Grass and 40 Twigs for basic tools.
  2. Find Flint to craft your first Pickaxe and Axe.
  3. Locate a reliable food source:
    1. Pick Berries from bushes.
    2. Find a Carrot field.
    3. Hunt small animals like Rabbits.

Nighttime Essentials:

  • Always have enough materials for a Torch.
  • Build a Campfire before total darkness falls.
    • Use Logs or Boards for longer burning.
    • Add Pinecones or Grass for a quick boost.
  • Stay close to the light to avoid Charlie's attack.

Core Mechanics to Watch:

The Darkness
The most dangerous enemy. Complete darkness will kill you instantly if you lack a light source.
Base Building
A central location with a Science Machine and a Fire Pit to store your items and cook food.
Seasons
The world changes over time. You must prepare for Winter's freezing cold and Summer's scorching heat.
